IELTS writing

Should employees choose where they work?

Writing task

Employers should allow employees to work from home whenever their duties can be carried out remotely. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include relevant examples. Write at least 250 words. Allow about 40 minutes.

Plan your response

  1. Choose a position. Does the ability to finish individual tasks at home settle the question?
  2. Develop one benefit for a worker and one cost for a team. Give a concrete example of each.
  3. Specify when an employer could reasonably require people to come in.
Read the model answer · 260 words

An accountant may need nothing more than a laptop to prepare a report, yet still need colleagues to explain an unfamiliar figure. For this reason, I broadly support giving employees a choice about working from home, provided that employers can require attendance for a clear purpose.

One advantage of this choice is that people can match their surroundings to the task. A designer preparing three alternative layouts, for example, might make better progress in a quiet room than at a desk where colleagues regularly stop to ask questions. Working at home also removes the journey to the office. Someone who spends an hour on the bus each morning can use part of that time to rest before starting work. Neither benefit depends on the worker having a particular family situation.

However, a task that looks independent on paper can involve considerable cooperation in practice. Imagine a new employee who has been asked to investigate why orders arrive late. Sitting beside an experienced colleague for an afternoon may reveal that two departments use the same term for different stages of delivery. The newcomer might never think to ask about this distinction in a scheduled video call. In that situation, bringing the relevant people together serves a specific learning goal.

I therefore favour a policy that starts with employee choice and makes room for exceptions. A manager requesting attendance should be able to explain what the team hopes to achieve together. That approach gives workers room to organise their day while ensuring that a colleague who needs help can actually receive it.

Try a rewrite

Rewrite the third paragraph from the perspective of an experienced employee training a newcomer. Use a different workplace example and explain exactly what the newcomer learns.
