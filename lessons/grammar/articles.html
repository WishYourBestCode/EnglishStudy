Grammar

Choosing articles

Use a or an for one unspecified singular countable thing. Use the when the listener can identify it. General plurals and uncountable nouns often take no article.

Compare the examples

  • A technician called. The technician asked about our printer.
  • Technicians repair printers. Both plural nouns refer to things in general.

Practice

Choose one answer for each question.

1. Choose a or an. Bring me ___ USB cable.

USB begins with the consonant sound in “you.”

2. Choose a or an. The update took ___ hour.

The h in hour is silent, so the first sound is a vowel.

3. Any one of the ten identical laptops will do. Bring me ___ laptop.

The speaker accepts any laptop. Laptop begins with a consonant sound.

4. I bought a desk and a chair. ___ chair has wheels.

The chair is identifiable because the speaker just mentioned it.

5. You mean all printers in general. ___ printers need maintenance.

The bare plural printers refers to the whole category.

6. You mean advice in general. ___ advice can be helpful.

Advice is uncountable. To count it, say a piece of advice.
