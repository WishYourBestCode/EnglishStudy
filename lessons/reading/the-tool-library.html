Reading

The tool library

Read this short report. For each statement, decide whether the writer confirms it, contradicts it, or gives too little information to decide.

On the ground floor of an old post office, Maple Street Tool Library lends tools to people who would rather borrow a ladder or a drill than buy one. At first, every item had to be brought back on Friday between two and five. The rule was easy for the volunteers to remember. For a member working a late shift, however, returning a hammer could mean taking an hour off work.

In September, the volunteers put a locked return box beside the bakery next door. Members could drop off small hand tools at any time. The change had nothing to do with damaged tools. It was a six-week trial to see whether longer return hours would help people meet the deadline. Ladders could not fit in the box, and all powered tools had to be handed to a volunteer so their condition could be checked.

In the six weeks before the trial, fourteen hand tools came back late. During the trial, five did. The coordinator welcomed the drop but pointed out that several members had been away that month. The team decided to leave the box in place through the winter and collect more information before making a decision about the following year.

Check the evidence

Choose False when the passage says the opposite. Choose Not given when you would need another fact to decide.

1. People initially had to return tools on Friday afternoons.

The original rule was “every item had to be brought back on Friday between two and five.” The phrase “at first” points to the initial rule.

2. The library introduced the box because several tools had been damaged.

The pilot addressed a scheduling problem. The passage explicitly says the change had nothing to do with damaged tools.

3. Cordless drills can be left in the return box if the battery is removed.

The report says that “all powered tools had to be handed to a volunteer.” A cordless drill is a powered tool even when its battery is removed.

4. More members borrowed tools during the pilot than in the previous six weeks.

The report counts late returns. It gives no figures for how many people borrowed tools in either period.

5. The team decided to keep the box for now.

The plan for the winter is to leave the box in place. A decision about the following year will come later.

6. A local business paid for the return box.

One business is mentioned as a neighbour. The passage gives no information about who paid for the box.
