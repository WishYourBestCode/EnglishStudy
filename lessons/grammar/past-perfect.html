Grammar

The past perfect

Use had + past participle to look back from one moment in the past to an earlier event. The form is the same for every subject.

Compare the examples

  • When I reached the platform, the train had left. I missed it.
  • When I reached the platform, the train left. Its departure happened as I arrived.

Practice

Choose one answer for each question.

1. The technician arrived at ten. The server had stopped at nine. Which happened first?

Had stopped describes the earlier event, at nine.

2. Complete the past perfect. By the time the meeting began, Jo ___ the report.

Had read shows that Jo finished reading before the meeting began.

3. Choose the negative past perfect. Before the crash, I ___ a backup.

Put not after had and use the past participle made.

4. Which past perfect question asks about a restart before the inspection?

Put had before the subject and the past participle restarted after it.

5. When we arrived, the guests had already ___.

The forms are go, went, gone. Use gone after had.

6. We tested the app at three and fixed the bug at four. Which sentence preserves that order?

Had already tested looks back from the later repair to the earlier test.
