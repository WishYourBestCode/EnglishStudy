How this practice works

Choose a range and category, then start the quiz. The program groups the phrases by category and places five phrases into each paragraph. Type only the missing word, such as of in “I am aware ___ the outage.”

Guided

The five complete phrases are listed above the paragraph. Match them to the correct sentences.

Recall

You see the five head words and meanings, but the paired words are hidden.

Challenge

The phrase bank is hidden. Answer from memory and context only.

Keyboard

Press Enter to move to the next blank. Press Enter on the final blank to check the paragraph.
