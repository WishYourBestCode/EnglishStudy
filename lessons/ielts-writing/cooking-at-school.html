IELTS writing

Does cooking belong on the school timetable?

Writing task

All secondary school students should learn to prepare basic meals as part of their education. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include relevant examples. Write at least 250 words. Allow about 40 minutes.

Plan your response

  1. Define the minimum skill every student should leave school with.
  2. Explain how a lesson could build that skill. Consider why learning at home might be difficult for some students.
  3. Address the strongest objection. How much timetable space does your proposal need?
Read the model answer · 262 words

A teenager can leave school able to solve a complex equation yet feel uncertain about turning a bag of lentils into dinner. I believe every secondary school student should receive some practical cooking instruction because the ability to prepare a few affordable meals is valuable regardless of future occupation.

School lessons would give all students a chance to practise a skill that is sometimes taken for granted at home. Even parents who cook regularly may have little time to let a beginner use the kitchen on a busy evening. In a classroom, students could work in pairs to prepare a simple soup. Each person would have to chop vegetables, judge when they were soft and taste the result before adding seasoning. These small decisions are difficult to learn simply by watching someone else make them.

The strongest objection concerns the timetable. Adding a weekly cooking class throughout secondary school would take time away from subjects that already struggle to cover their material. However, basic competence need not require years of instruction. A short course could teach students to prepare three meals from inexpensive ingredients. For the final exercise, each pair could adapt one recipe to a fixed budget and cook it without following the original instructions word for word. This would test whether students could use what they had learned independently.

In my view, this limited commitment is justified. School cannot teach every household skill, but a student who can safely prepare a meal and adjust it to the money available has gained a practical freedom that will matter almost every day.

Try a rewrite

Rewrite the timetable paragraph for a school with only one small kitchen. Give a realistic class arrangement and show how each student would get hands-on practice.
