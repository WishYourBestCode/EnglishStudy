Three patterns to remember

Learn the small group of words together, then change the rest of the sentence.

1. Verb + preposition

Remember the verb and its usual preposition as a pair.

I believe in my team.

I’m waiting for your reply.

Try to remember wait for, not just wait.

2. Verb + -ing

Some verbs are followed directly by an -ing form.

I’ll keep working.

I finished checking.

Keep working has no preposition.

3. To + base verb

After need to or plan to, use the base verb.

I need to check the settings.

I plan to call the user.

Use check here, not checking.

When a verb follows a preposition, use -ing: thank you for helping; think of calling; before restarting.

In look forward to hearing, to is a preposition. In need to hear, it is part of the infinitive pattern.

Try restarting means testing a possible fix. Try to restart means making an effort to restart.
