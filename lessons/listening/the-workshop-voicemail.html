Listening

The workshop voicemail

A community centre has changed the arrangements for a workshop. Listen once for the main change. On the second listen, write down the time, room, and one thing to bring.

Open the audio file · Practice voicemail read by a synthesized voice.

What did you hear?

1. What time does the workshop now start?

Maya gives the new start time first. Nine thirty was the original time, and eleven thirty is the finish.

2. Which entrance should participants use?

Maya directs participants to the garden entrance because the main doors are being painted.

3. Where will the group meet?

Listen for both pieces of information in “room six, on the ground floor.”

4. What should participants bring?

The centre supplies the bikes and tools. Gloves are the only item participants need to bring.

5. When should someone call the centre?

People who can attend do not need to reply. Anyone who cannot attend should contact the centre by Friday afternoon.

Read the transcript

Hi, this is Maya from Brookside Community Centre. I'm calling about the bike repair workshop you booked. You may still have the old email, so let me give you the new details.

We're still meeting on Saturday, but the workshop will start at ten thirty in the morning. Nine thirty was the original start time. The session takes one hour, and we'll finish at eleven thirty.

Come to room six, on the ground floor. The main doors are being painted, so please use the garden entrance. You'll see it just past the bus stop.

We'll supply the bikes and all the tools. The only thing you need to bring is a pair of gloves. If these arrangements suit you, there's no need to call us back. If you can't come, please let us know by Friday afternoon so we can offer your place to someone else. Thanks!
